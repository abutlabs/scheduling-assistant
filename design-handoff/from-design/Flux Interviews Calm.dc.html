1Flux Interviews — calm redesign, first pass

How to read this page. There's nothing to compare or pick here. It's one proposed direction for the five screens you asked for. Each block (1a–1e) is one screen: the top row is light mode (desktop and phone), the row below is dark mode. 1f is the token sheet. Zoom out to see everything at once, and tell me what to change.

The system. Warm paper background, white surfaces, one blue for "do this", orange-red only for Report. Inter Tight at light weights for big headings, Roboto Mono only for dates and counts. Every screen leads with one large next step; everything else sits below in quiet rows or is folded into a disclosure.

Assumptions. "Run view" is renamed "Today". The test-mode banner becomes a small pill in the bar. The view switcher (Admin / Advisor / Team lead / Developer) and Mock move into the name menu at top right, so the bar only holds navigation, the campaign and Report. Team leads get the same home as advisors; only the round name changes.
